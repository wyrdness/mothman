## Overview

The Mothman is a cryptid that reportedly appeared in Point Pleasant, West Virginia and surrounding areas between November 1966 and December 1967. Witnesses described a large, man-sized or larger creature with enormous wings and glowing red eyes.

The Mothman phenomenon became inextricably linked to the collapse of the Silver Bridge on December 15, 1967, which killed 46 people. This association has led to Mothman's reputation as an omen or harbinger of disaster. The town of Point Pleasant has embraced the legend with a museum, 12-foot statue, and annual festival.

## Notable Sightings

### Scarberry-Mallette Sighting (1966) ⭐

> **Date**: November 15, 1966
> **Location**: TNT Area, Point Pleasant
> **Witnesses**: 4

Two couples - Roger and Linda Scarberry and Steve and Mary Mallette - were driving near the TNT area around midnight when they saw a large gray creature with glowing red eyes. The creature had wings folded against its back. When they fled, it pursued their car at speeds over 100 mph, flying without flapping its wings. They drove directly to the Mason County courthouse to report the incident.

**Credibility Assessment**: Medium — Four consistent witnesses; immediate police report; witnesses maintained account for decades.

---

### Thomas Ury Highway Sighting (1966)

> **Date**: November 25, 1966
> **Location**: Route 62, near TNT Area
> **Witnesses**: 1

Thomas Ury was driving in early morning when he spotted a large gray figure in a field. The creature spread wings and flew straight up, then circled his car and flew alongside as he accelerated to 70+ mph.

## The Silver Bridge Connection

On December 15, 1967, the Silver Bridge connecting Point Pleasant to Gallipolis, Ohio collapsed during rush hour traffic, killing 46 people. The bridge failure was later attributed to a defective eyebar in the suspension chain.

However, Mothman sightings largely ceased after the collapse, leading to speculation that:
- Mothman was warning of the impending disaster
- The events were somehow connected
- The creature departed after the tragedy

This association cemented Mothman's reputation as a harbinger of disaster.

## Theories & Explanations

### Cryptozoological Theories

1. **Interdimensional Being**
   John Keel proposed Mothman as an entity from another dimension, connected to the UFO activity reported in the area during the same period.

2. **Harbinger/Omen**
   Mothman as a warning entity appearing before disasters.

### Skeptical Explanations

1. **Sandhill Crane**
   Dr. Robert L. Smith (WVU) suggested misidentified sandhill cranes, which have 7-foot wingspans and reddish coloring around their eyes.

2. **Large Owl**
   Barred owl or great horned owl with eyeshine from car headlights.

3. **Mass Hysteria**
   Initial misidentification led to panic and suggestibility, causing more "sightings."
